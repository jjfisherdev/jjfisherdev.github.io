How a round works

  1. A real question appears

    "Height of the Eiffel Tower in feet?" Nobody knows the exact number, and that's the point.

  2. Bet how close you can get

    Type a margin: "±300" means your guess will land within 300 feet of the answer. Smaller is bolder.

  3. Squeeze it or call the bluff

    Your rival squeezes the bet tighter (and takes it over), or holds CALL BLUFF to make you prove it.

  4. Place your window

    The forced player slides a window of their guess ± the bet along the bridge. The other calls the answer ABOVE or BELOW it.

  5. The truth lands

    Inside the window, the guesser wins the round. Outside, on the called side, the caller wins. Best of 1, 3 or 5.

Two ways to play

2 players, one phone

Pass-and-play for couples, friends and family game night. A full-screen card says whose turn it is at every hand-off, so nothing is ever hidden from the wrong player.

VS AI, solo

Easy, Medium or Hard. The AI never sees the answer: it makes its own estimate, squeezes your bet while it trusts it and calls your bluff when your bet gets too tight. The level only changes how close its guess lands. Your turn has no timer.

Offline anywhere

Every question ships inside the app, so it plays on planes, road trips and the subway. Near You mode picks questions about places around you, worked out on your phone.

Questions people ask

What is Squeeze?

Squeeze: The Estimation Duel is a two-player trivia game about numbers for iPhone and iPad, made by JJ Fisher LLC. Instead of knowing the exact answer, you bet how close your guess will land and dare your rival to squeeze that bet tighter or call your bluff.

Can two people play on one phone?

Yes. Squeeze is built for pass-and-play: two players share one iPhone or iPad and hand it back and forth. No second device, no accounts and no internet connection are needed.

Can I play alone?

Yes. VS AI is a one-player mode against a computer opponent with three levels (Easy, Medium, Hard). The AI never sees the answer; the level only sets how accurate its own estimate is.

Does it work offline, without Wi-Fi?

Yes. All 7,808 questions are inside the app, so the game plays fully offline, on planes, road trips and the subway. Ads need a connection; without one, the game simply carries on.

Is it a good game for couples, date night or family game night?

That's what it was made for: one phone, two players, best of 1, 3 or 5 rounds, and a story at every reveal. Questions are suitable for all ages (rated 4+).

Is there an iPhone game like Wits & Wagers?

If you like estimation trivia, where being close is what counts, Squeeze is a head-to-head take on it for two players on one phone, with betting on your margin and bluffing. It is a different game, not affiliated with Wits & Wagers or North Star Games.

Is Squeeze free?

Yes, free with ads: one at the end of every second or third match (never during a turn), plus one when you change the question more than twice in a match. There are no purchases. Ads are non-personalized, and Squeeze never asks to track you.

Which devices and languages?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, in English and French. Squeeze is not available on Android.

How do I learn the rules?

The first time you open Squeeze, the fox and the dog play one round for you, without a word. Tap WATCH on the home screen to see it again.
