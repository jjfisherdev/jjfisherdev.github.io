Descriptions

One line

Squeeze is a two-player estimation and bluff game for iPhone: share one phone, bet how close you can guess real numbers, and squeeze the bet or call the bluff.

Short (50 words)

Squeeze: The Estimation Duel turns numeric trivia into a head-to-head bluffing game. Two players share one iPhone or iPad. Nobody knows the exact answer, so you bet how close you can get; your rival squeezes the bet tighter or calls your bluff. Free, offline, with a solo VS AI mode.

Long

Most trivia rewards knowing the answer. Squeeze rewards knowing how well you know it. A real question appears ("Height of the Eiffel Tower in feet?"), and the first player bets a margin: their guess will land within ±300 feet. The rival can squeeze that bet tighter, taking it over, or call the bluff. The forced player places a window on a 3D bridge, the other calls above or below, and the true number drops in. Squeeze plays fully offline on one device, in English or French, with 7,808 questions across seven categories. Playing alone, VS AI offers three levels; the AI never sees the answer, and the level only changes how close its own estimate lands.
